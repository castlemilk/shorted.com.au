[Telix](/shorts/TLX) was **10.75%** shorted on **28 September**, according to ASIC’s daily aggregate report. [ref-1] BiPASS gained FDA Fast Track designation two days later. The position report stops before that news.

Telix's **30 September** release concerns gallium-68 PSMA-PET imaging alongside MRI before a prostate biopsy. This proposed use remains investigational and has no FDA or other regulatory approval. [ref-2]

Fast Track can mean more frequent FDA contact and rolling review for programs addressing serious conditions and unmet needs. Other expedited pathways have their own eligibility criteria. The designation supplies no approval decision or commercial launch date. [ref-3]

BiPASS had already completed Phase 3 enrolment, disclosed in a **2 September** ASX announcement filed with the SEC. The filing also describes FDA alignment on an NDA pathway. [ref-4] Enrolment puts patients into the study; it does not supply a successful readout.

Telix says it plans an NDA submission through a new-product registration pathway. Approval of Illuccix and Gozellix for existing uses does not cover this pre-biopsy setting. [ref-2] Study evidence, submission and the regulator's decision remain separate steps. Adoption and commercial returns would need evidence of their own.

ASIC's aggregate short reports arrive **T+4**. The quoted figure comes from its 28 September report. [ref-5][ref-1] It establishes neither a short seller's motive nor post-designation covering. The [squeeze mechanics](/blog/short-squeeze-explained) require more than a crowded position.
