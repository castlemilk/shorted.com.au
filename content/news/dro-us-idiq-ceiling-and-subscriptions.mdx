The **15.17%** short position came first. [DroneShield](/shorts/DRO) was at that level in ASIC’s **28 September** report, before the procurement announcement and service launch. [ref-1]

On **30 September**, DroneShield announced a three-year IDIQ procurement vehicle for JIATF-401's Domestic Shield initiative, with a **US$500M** ceiling. Material orders will be disclosed separately. [ref-2]

US$500M is a ceiling with three years attached. It gives neither order values nor delivery dates. Orders, delivery and reported revenue are separate milestones; the ceiling cannot be booked as a sale.

Mission Ready Services followed on **1 October**: annually renewable software updates, training and support. Existing software subscribers move across at renewal. DroneShield says more than **4,100 software-enabled devices** are deployed. That is a device count, not a disclosed count of paying service subscribers. [ref-3]

Paid adoption, renewals and reported service revenue would give the device figure some commercial weight. The launch establishes the offering; those results still need disclosure.

ASIC publishes aggregate positions **T+4**, without identifying the short sellers. The quoted figures come from its 28 September report. [ref-4][ref-1] It reveals no trader motive or post-news covering. A [short squeeze](/blog/short-squeeze-explained) needs more evidence than one percentage.
