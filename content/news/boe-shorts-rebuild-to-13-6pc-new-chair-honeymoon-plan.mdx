[Boss Energy](/shorts/BOE) was **13.58%** shorted on **30 September**, 56,391,194 of 415,198,998 shares in ASIC's daily aggregate file. Among ordinary shares in that file, only [DroneShield](/shorts/DRO) at 15.07% sits higher. [ref-1] Shorted's four-week change for BOE is +3.67 percentage points, the largest build among the 16 stocks shorted above 10%.

The position has made more than a round trip. It was 11.96% on **1 July** [ref-2], fell to **8.46%** on **26 August** [ref-3], and stood at 10.09% on **17 September**. [ref-4] The next day's file showed **12.75%**: 52,922,724 shares against 41,895,507 a day earlier, an increase of 11,027,217 shares in one position date. [ref-5] That jump came three days after the company's 15 September Market Day webcast; the file records the position, not the reason. [ref-6]

Shorted puts BOE's [days to cover](/blog/days-to-cover-asx) at 7.8. In the [W40 report](/reports/weekly/10-most-shorted-asx-stocks-week-40-2026), BOE was third at 13.19% on 28 September, behind Lotus at 13.26%. Two position dates later it had passed Lotus.

The August low came the day before the company's **27 August** results and new Honeymoon feasibility study. The study plans about **13.8 million pounds** of drummed U₃O₈ over a nine-year mine life running to FY2035, peaking at 1.9 million pounds a year from FY2030, with a life-of-mine C1 cost averaging $50/lb. FY2026 production was 1.407 million pounds at a C1 cost of $39/lb, and the year ended with $207.3 million in cash and liquid assets, $116.3 million of it uranium inventory at book value, and no debt. [ref-7]

Honeymoon's own resource is 20.8 million pounds. Gould's Dam (33.1 million pounds) and Jasons (12.0 million pounds) sit outside the study, "subject to separate studies". [ref-7] At the Market Day, management framed the two deposits as options to evaluate rather than committed projects, inside a plan for a larger, lower-cost and longer-life business. [ref-8]

Peter Botten commenced as Chair on **30 September**, succeeding Wyatt Buck, who stays on the board as an independent non-executive director. Botten was Managing Director of Oil Search from 1994 to 2020; the transition was first disclosed on 3 July. [ref-9]

Macquarie Group became a substantial holder on **22 September** with 21,276,954 shares, or 5.12%, under a notice dated 25 September. Of those, 12,614,905 are controlled by Macquarie Bank through stock borrowing and lending arrangements. [ref-10] It is a disclosure of control, not of any short position, Macquarie's or anyone else's.

[Lotus Resources](/shorts/LOT) was 13.53%, [Paladin Energy](/shorts/PDN) 11.36% and [Deep Yellow](/shorts/DYL) 8.65% on 30 September. [ref-1] Paladin's shares were down around 17% in the week to 16 September. [ref-11] A later report tied the slide to a research house that did not expect Langer Heinrich to run at its full nameplate rate for an entire year and trimmed its longer-term production forecasts. [ref-12]

<PullQuote>A reported position is a count, not a motive.</PullQuote>

ASIC publishes aggregate positions **T+4**, and the reports do not contain short seller details. [ref-13] A reported position is a count, not a motive. It cannot separate a bearish view from a hedge. A crowded position is not a [squeeze](/blog/short-squeeze-explained); that needs buying the file cannot see. The denominator moved too, by 23,678 shares between the 18 and 30 September files, which barely touches the percentage. [ref-5] [ref-1]
