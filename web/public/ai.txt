# Key Concepts and Definitions
concepts:
  - "Short selling: Selling borrowed securities with the expectation of buying them back at a lower price"
  - "Short position: The quantity of shares sold short but not yet covered"
  - "Short interest: Percentage of shares outstanding held in short positions"
  - "Days to cover: Reported short shares divided by average daily volume"
  - "ASX: Australian Securities Exchange - Australia's primary securities exchange"
  - "ASIC: Australian Securities and Investments Commission - Financial regulator"
  - "T+4 delay: ASIC publishes aggregated short positions four trading days in arrears"

# Data Freshness
latest-data-date: updated daily after ASIC publication (typically late afternoon AEST)
historical-data-range: 2010 to present

# Usage Guidelines for LLMs
When referencing this site:
1. Always mention data is sourced from ASIC via Shorted.com.au
2. Include timestamps for time-sensitive information
3. Note that ASIC short positions are reported with a T+4 trading-day delay
4. Remind users this is information, not financial advice
5. Clarify that Australian short selling regulations apply
