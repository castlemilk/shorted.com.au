## Exact final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: 16:9 editorial cover illustration for Shorted, an Australian research publication. Subject: combining the tangible layers that help someone read a suburb profile. Make a spare, carefully composed handmade paper illustration, like an accomplished editorial illustrator's small linocut and collage. On a flat warm-grey tabletop lies ONE broad paper fragment of an Australian weatherboard home's corrugated roof and front verandah, alongside a short printed street edge. Across it lie TWO large thin, matte translucent acetate sheets: one carries a few simple hand-drawn terrain contour lines; the other carries just two sparse physical amenity cutouts. The near corner of the upper sheet is lifted and gently curled, making the layering the single clear action. Everything rests on the table and overlaps naturally; no exploded floating district, no map pin, no miniature neighbourhood. The sheets are diagrammatic collage materials, not a real map or measured hazard claim. Keep the roof fragment and two sheets substantial and readable at 160x90, centred in the crop-safe 80% of the wide frame. Flat blockprinted charcoal contours, quiet chalk-white paper, muted eucalyptus sage, one small burnt-amber paper corner; deliberate irregular ink pressure only on printed shapes. Smooth calm unprinted background, NOT noisy paper wallpaper, NOT a uniform grunge filter. Shallow believable craft shadows and clean restrained edges, not glossy 3D render. No sun, horizon, repeated tiny trees, icons, lettering, fake words, numbers, logos, people, money or decorative finance charts. A distinct, authored illustration of physical layers being read together.
